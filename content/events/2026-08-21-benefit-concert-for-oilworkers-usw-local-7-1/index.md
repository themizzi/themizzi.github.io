The Effigies, The Bollweevils, and The Rumble will perform at this benefit concert for Oilworkers USW Local 7-1 at Avenue 912.

USW Local 7-1 union members get in free with a union card. All door proceeds benefit the USW Local 7-1 strike/lockout fund. This is an all-ages show.
