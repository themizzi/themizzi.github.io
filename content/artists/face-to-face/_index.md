Face to Face is a Southern California punk rock band formed in 1991 in Victorville. They broke through in the 1990s with melodic, fast-paced songs and became widely known for "Disconnected" from *Big Choice*.

After a 2004 breakup, the band reunited in 2008 and has remained active with releases including *Protection* and *No Way Out but Through*.
