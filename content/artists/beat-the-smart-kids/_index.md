Beat The Smart Kids is a Chicago ska-punk band formed by local scene veterans.

The band features former and current members of Waste Basket, The Indecisives, Still Alive, and The Damn Tracks, blending catchy horn riffs with gritty vocals and a driving punk rhythm.
