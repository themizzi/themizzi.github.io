Sparta is an El Paso, Texas rock band formed in 2001 by Jim Ward, Paul Hinojos, and Tony Hajjar after the first breakup of At the Drive-In. Their sound blends post-hardcore intensity with atmospheric and melodic guitar-driven songwriting.

The band debuted with *Wiretap Scars* in 2002 and later released *Porcelain*, *Threes*, *Trust the River*, and *Sparta*, followed by *Cut a Silhouette* in 2026.
