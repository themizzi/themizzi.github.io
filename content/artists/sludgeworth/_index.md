Sludgeworth is a Chicago punk rock band formed in 1989 by members of Screeching Weasel, including Dan Schafer and Brian Vermin. The band released the self-titled EP in 1990 and the LP *What's This?* in 1991 before splitting in the early 1990s.

Their material was later compiled as *Losers of the Year*, and the band returned for select reunion performances before becoming active again in the 2020s, releasing new music on Red Scare Industries.
